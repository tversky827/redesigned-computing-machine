Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about selling your diamond to Diamonds2Dollars. Still have a question? Email offers@diamonds2dollars.com.

Is a valuation really free?

Yes. Submitting your diamond for a valuation is completely free and carries no obligation. You only sell if you're happy with our final offer.

How does the process work?

Four steps: (1) submit the form with your details and photos; (2) we review it and email you a cash offer; (3) if you accept, we send you a prepaid, insured shipping label; (4) we confirm the piece in person and pay you. If you don't like the final offer, we send it back and pay the return shipping.

How do you pay me?

We pay by Zelle or check — your choice — after we receive your item and confirm it matches what was described.

Who pays for shipping?

We do. Once you accept an offer, we email you a prepaid, fully insured shipping label, so it costs you nothing and your item is protected in transit.

What if I don't accept the final offer?

No problem and no cost. If our final offer changes after inspection and you'd rather keep your item, we return it to you and pay the return shipping ourselves.

Is the online offer final?

The offer we email is based on the details and photos you provide. We confirm the final offer once we inspect the piece in person. If anything differs from the description, we'll let you know — and you're always free to decline.

Do I need a GIA or other certificate?

No. A lab certificate (GIA, IGI, AGS, etc.) helps us give a more precise offer, but it isn't required. If you don't have one, just choose “Not sure” on the form and we'll assess it ourselves.

What kinds of items do you buy?

Diamonds of all shapes and sizes — loose stones or diamonds set in rings, earrings, necklaces, pendants, and bracelets. When in doubt, submit it and we'll take a look.

How long does it take?

We review submissions promptly and typically email an offer within a business day or two. After you ship, payment goes out once we receive and confirm the piece.

Do I need to prove I own the item?

Yes. You must be the legal owner with the right to sell, and we may ask for identification or proof of ownership. We do not purchase stolen or misrepresented goods.

Is my information private?

Yes. Your details and photos are used only to evaluate your item and communicate with you. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.
