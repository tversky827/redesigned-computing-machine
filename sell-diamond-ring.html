Whether it's an heirloom, an upgrade, or a ring you no longer wear, we make selling simple. Tell us about the ring, add photos of the stone and setting, and we'll email you a fair offer.

Accepted offers ship free and fully insured. We confirm the ring with our gemologists and pay you quickly — and if you'd rather keep it, we send it back at our expense.

Helpful details to include

  • The center diamond's approximate carat and the 4 Cs
  • Metal type (platinum, 18K/14K gold) and any designer or brand
  • Any accent or side stones
  • Certificates, receipts, or an appraisal, if you have them
