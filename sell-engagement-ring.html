Sell an Engagement Ring

A private, respectful, and fair way to sell an engagement ring when you're ready to move on.

Get My Offer

Selling an engagement ring can be a big step. We keep it private and straightforward: submit the ring's details and photos, and receive a fair, no-obligation offer by email — on your own time, with no pressure.

If you accept, shipping is prepaid, insured, and overnight. Our gemologists confirm the ring and we pay you by Zelle or check. Your information stays confidential throughout.

What to have ready

  • The center diamond's carat and, if known, the 4 Cs
  • Metal and setting style
  • Original receipt, appraisal, or GIA/IGI report, if available
  • A few clear photos in good light
