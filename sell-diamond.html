Get My Offer

Selling a diamond shouldn't mean driving around to pawn shops or accepting a lowball on the spot. Submit your diamond's details and a few photos, and our team reviews it and emails you a fair, market-based offer.

If you accept, we send a prepaid, insured, overnight shipping label. Our gemologists confirm the diamond, and we pay you promptly by Zelle or check. If it isn't the right fit, we return it and cover the return shipping.

What affects a diamond's value

  • Carat weight — larger diamonds are worth more per carat
  • Cut, color, and clarity — the other three of the 4 Cs
  • Shape and whether it's natural or lab-grown
  • A lab report (GIA, IGI, AGS) — helpful but not required
