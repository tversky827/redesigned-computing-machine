How Much Is My Diamond Worth?

The honest answer: it depends on the 4 Cs and today's market. Here's how to find out for free.

Get My Offer

A diamond's resale value comes down to carat weight and the other three Cs — cut, color, and clarity — plus current market demand for that combination. Two diamonds of the same size can be worth very different amounts.

The simplest way to know is to get a real offer. Submit your diamond's details and photos and we'll email you a market-based figure — free, with no obligation to sell.

The 4 Cs, briefly

  • Carat — the diamond's weight
  • Cut — how well it's proportioned and how it sparkles
  • Color — how colorless it is (D is top)
  • Clarity — how free it is of inclusions
